INDUSTRIAL PUMP ALARM TROUBLESHOOTING SOP
 
Version: 1.0
 
Section 1: Temperature Alarm
 
For a high temperature alarm:
 
1. Confirm the alarm code.
2. Check current temperature.
3. Check cooling system operation.
4. Check lubrication.
5. Check bearing condition.
6. If temperature continues to increase, stop the equipment according to safety procedures.
 
Section 2: Vibration Alarm
 
For a high vibration alarm:
 
1. Confirm vibration alarm.
2. Check bearing condition.
3. Check shaft or coupling alignment.
4. Check mounting or foundation bolts.
5. Inspect rotor balance.
 
Section 3: Safety
 
Do not perform maintenance on rotating equipment while it is operating.
 
Follow site lockout/tagout procedures before physical inspection.
 
If the cause of the alarm cannot be determined, escalate the incident to the maintenance engineering team.
