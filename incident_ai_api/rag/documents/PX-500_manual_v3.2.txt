PX-500 INDUSTRIAL PUMP
Maintenance Manual
Version: 3.2
 
Section 1: High Temperature Alarm
 
Alarm Code: TEMP-HIGH
 
When the pump temperature exceeds the normal operating range, the TEMP-HIGH alarm is triggered.
 
Possible causes:
1. Insufficient cooling flow
2. Low lubrication level
3. Bearing overheating
4. Blocked cooling passage
5. Excessive pump load
 
Recommended checks:
1. Check cooling water flow.
2. Inspect the cooling passage for blockage.
3. Check bearing temperature.
4. Check lubrication level.
5. Verify that the pump is not operating above its rated load.
 
For PX-500 pumps, the normal bearing temperature should remain below 80 degrees Celsius.
 
If bearing temperature exceeds 80 degrees Celsius, stop the pump and inspect the bearing and lubrication system.
 
Section 2: Vibration Alarm
 
Alarm Code: VIB-HIGH
 
Possible causes:
1. Bearing wear
2. Shaft misalignment
3. Loose mounting bolts
4. Rotor imbalance
 
Recommended checks:
1. Inspect bearing condition.
2. Check shaft alignment.
3. Check mounting bolts.
4. Inspect rotor balance.
 
Section 3: Preventive Maintenance
 
Perform the following checks during preventive maintenance:
 
1. Inspect bearings.
2. Check lubrication.
3. Inspect cooling system.
4. Check mounting bolts.
5. Record vibration and temperature readings.
