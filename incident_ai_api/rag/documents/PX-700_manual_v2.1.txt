PX-700 INDUSTRIAL PUMP
Maintenance Manual
Version: 2.1
 
Section 1: High Temperature Alarm
 
Alarm Code: TEMP-HIGH
 
When the pump temperature exceeds the normal operating range, the TEMP-HIGH alarm is triggered.
 
Possible causes:
1. Cooling fan failure
2. High ambient temperature
3. Bearing friction
4. Excessive pump load
 
Recommended checks:
1. Check cooling fan operation.
2. Check ambient temperature.
3. Inspect bearing condition.
4. Verify pump load.
 
For PX-700 pumps, the normal bearing temperature should remain below 90 degrees Celsius.
 
If bearing temperature exceeds 90 degrees Celsius, stop the pump and inspect the bearing.
 
Section 2: Vibration Alarm
 
Alarm Code: VIB-HIGH
 
Possible causes:
1. Coupling misalignment
2. Bearing damage
3. Rotor imbalance
4. Loose foundation bolts
 
Recommended checks:
1. Inspect coupling alignment.
2. Check bearing condition.
3. Inspect rotor balance.
4. Check foundation bolts.
 
Section 3: Preventive Maintenance
 
Perform the following checks:
 
1. Inspect bearings.
2. Check cooling fan.
3. Check coupling alignment.
4. Record vibration and temperature readings.
